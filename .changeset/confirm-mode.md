Threads say who acts on them next, and the review script gains a read-only confirm mode. Every thread the review opens starts `Fix required:` (the fix run that follows takes it as work) or `Decision required:` (written to the developer, who answers in the thread), a body with neither getting `Fix required:` put in front, and the review answers no existing thread: a point an open thread covers is skipped. `address-review` is given only the threads that are its work, which leaves out a `Decision required:` thread until the developer answers it and those a bot's last word closed, so a spec gap the review raises for the developer is never declined by the fixer before they decide. `REVIEW_MODE=confirm` runs the review script on the threads under confirmation and judges each on HEAD (checks pass, a test exercises the thread's point and fails without the fix, the behaviour holds), writing `verdicts.json` with a `Fix verified.` or `Fix not verified:` reply per thread for the host to post, and to resolve on a verified thread of the bot's own; a commit in confirm mode fails the run, and a fault in the fix commits is a `Decision required:` thread. The openers and the thread rules live in `review/threads.ts`.
